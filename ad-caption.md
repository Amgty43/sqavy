## Notes on the copy

- The hook is loss, not features. Contractors already know they miss calls; the ad names the cost.
- "25 seconds" and the $180–$250 quote range come straight from the demo — keep them consistent
  everywhere so the ad and the product tell the same story.
- The proof is the demo itself. Every caption points there rather than trying to explain the flow.
- No mention of AI. The buyer cares that the job got booked, not what booked it.
