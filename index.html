FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this replace my business phone number?

No — Sqavy works alongside your existing number. Missed calls trigger an automatic text from a dedicated line, so your main number stays the same.

What if I'm already on a call when another one comes in?

Sqavy only steps in when a call actually goes unanswered, so it never interrupts a call you're already on.

Do my customers need to install an app?

No. Everything happens over regular text messages — nothing for your customers to download.

Can I customize what Sqavy says?

Yes — the qualifying questions, quote ranges, and tone can be tailored to your business and trade.

What happens after a lead is qualified?

Sqavy sends a quote range and open calendar slots, and the booking lands directly on your calendar once the customer picks a time.
